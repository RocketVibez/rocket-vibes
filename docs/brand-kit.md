# Rocket Vibes — Brand Kit (v0.1)

## Positioning
High-energy streetwear for people who move like they’re already mid-launch.
Not corporate aerospace. Not meme-slop. Bold, loud, wearable.

**Working motto:** Launch loud.

## Voice
Short. Confident. A little reckless.
We don’t explain the vibe — we wear it.
Avoid: corporate speak, over-serious space jargon, soft wellness tone.

## Visual system
- **Bases:** near-black (#0B0B0F), charcoal (#1A1A22), deep navy (#0E1628)
- **Accents:** electric lime (#C8FF3D), hot magenta (#FF2D95), rocket orange (#FF5A1F)
- **Type:** heavy condensed sans for wordmarks; clean grotesque for body
- **Motifs:** simplified rocket silhouette, motion streaks, star punctures, bold wordmarks
- **Rule:** max 2 accent colors per piece; big type beats busy illustration

## First drop (when store is greenlit)
1. Classic tee — front wordmark, small back rocket mark
2. Oversized hoodie — chest hit + sleeve stripe
3. Dad hat — embroidered wordmark
