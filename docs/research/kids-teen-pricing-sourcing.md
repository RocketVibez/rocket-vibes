# Rocket Vibez: Kids & Teen Tee Sourcing and Pricing

**Store:** [rocketvibez.grok.me](https://rocketvibez.grok.me), a preview shop with no payments taken yet.
**What it sells today:**
- 20 prints plus a "metal drop", in black or white.
- Kids tees in sizes 4–14 for **$18**.
- Teen tees in S–XL for **$22**.
- Adult tees in XS–3X for **$26**.
- Custom graphics add **$10** (a kids custom tee is $28).
- Hoodies are mentioned, but there's no price. Tee + hoodie saves **$6**.
- Free shipping over **$60**.
- Caps, totes, pins, stickers and socks are paused.

**Research date:** Oct 14, 2026. Printful, Printify, ShirtSpace, Apliiq, competitor and Ad Express prices were pulled that day. A few figures carry over from the Oct 3 research and are labelled "Oct 3": Printful shipping rates, USPS rates, Bulk-Caps, the French Press Custom price table (updated Oct 2) and the Printify adult hoodie prices. Every price links to its source.

**Definitions used below**
- **Gross margin (GM)** = (price − product cost) ÷ price.
- **After card fee** = price − cost − Shopify's 2.9% + 30¢ card fee ([shopify.com/pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)). Use this when the customer pays shipping.
- **Free-ship contribution** = price − cost − fee − print-on-demand (POD) shipping. Use this when you absorb shipping.

> **Sizing watch-out:** the store sells kids sizes 4–14.
> - **Gildan 5000B and Gildan 18500B cover it.** Their XS = 4, S = 6–8, M = 10–12, L = 14–16 and XL = 18–20 ([S&S 5000B](https://www.ssactivewear.com/p/gildan/5000b), [S&S 18500B](https://www.ssactivewear.com/p/gildan/18500b)).
> - **Bella+Canvas 3001Y doesn't.** It starts at S = 6–8, so there's no size 4 ([S&S 3001Y spec](https://www.ssactivewear.com/ShopNow/ItemSpecSheet.aspx?ID=2209&LanguageCode=en)).

## TL;DR

- **At today's prices the $18 kids tee barely makes money.**
  - Printful (Gildan 5000B): **$9.88** cost, **45.1% GM**.
  - Add Printful's $4.69 kids-tee shipping and the card fee, and you keep **$2.61 (14.5%)**.
  - The Bella+Canvas 3001Y on Printful costs $13.76 and **loses $1.27** when you cover shipping.
- **Printify's Gildan 5000B is the cheapest kids-tee option.** It costs **$7.13**, or $6.76 on Premium, with US shipping from **$3.99**. At $18 that leaves $6.06 (33.7%) with free shipping.
- **Bulk screen printing doesn't fix the kids tee at these volumes.** The blank is cheap ($2.51), but 20 prints in 2 colors and ~6 sizes means very little volume per design. USPS shipping ($7.46) also costs more than POD shipping. Covering shipping on an $18 tee leaves between −$5.09 and +$3.44.
- **Recommended prices:**
  - Kids tee **$22**, teen tee **$26**, adult tee **$30**.
  - Keep custom at **+$10**: kids custom **$32**, teen **$36**, adult **$40**.
  - Youth hoodie **$42**, adult/teen hoodie **$48**.
  - Bundles stay at "$6 off": kids **$58**, teen **$68**, adult **$72**.
  - Accessories: cap **$28**, tote **$22**, enamel pin **$10** (or a 5-button set for **$14**), sticker **$4** (3 for $10), socks **$14**. Sell pins, stickers and socks mainly as add-ons.
- **Compliance applies (sizes 4–14 are "children's products").** You need a Children's Product Certificate (CPC), tracking labels, a 100 ppm lead limit on the garment, a 90 ppm limit on screen-print ink (a surface coating), and the clothing flammability rule. **No hood drawstrings on youth hoodies in sizes 2T–12.** See section 6.

## 6. Kids-product compliance (US): official sources

These rules apply because kids sizes 4–14 are designed mainly for children 12 and under. Teen and adult sizes generally aren't "children's products" unless they're marketed to that age group.

1. **Children's Product Certificate (CPC).** Every children's product subject to a CPSC rule needs one, based on testing at a CPSC-accepted lab. For screen-printed children's clothing, CPSC's own example certifies to three rules ([CPSC CPC example: clothing](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Testing-Certification/Childrens-Product-Certificate/CPC-Example-Clothing)):
   - **15 U.S.C. §1278a:** total lead content.
   - **16 CFR 1303:** paint and similar surface coatings (screen-print ink).
   - **16 CFR 1610:** flammability of clothing textiles.

   Per that same page, you can rely on component CPCs from your blank or ink suppliers, but **"you must still issue a CPC for the finished product."** Small-batch manufacturers who register with CPSC get some testing relief and must list their ID.
2. **Lead.** More than **100 ppm** lead in an accessible component makes a children's product a banned hazardous substance. Some untreated textiles, including dyed cotton, are exempt from *testing* but still have to comply and be certified ([CPSC total lead content](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Lead/Total-Lead-Content)). Screen prints and transfers are *not* covered by the exemption; the eCFR text of [16 CFR 1500.91](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-II/subchapter-C/part-1500/section-1500.91) excludes "inks used in after-treatment applications, including screen prints, transfers."
3. **Tracking labels.** Every children's product and its packaging needs permanent, visible marks showing: the manufacturer or private-labeler name, the location and date of production, and batch/run (cohort) information. A code is allowed if the consumer knows whom to contact ([CPSC tracking-label guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/tracking-label)). With POD, you need a printed inside label or tag (Printful inside label is +$0.99) that carries Rocket Vibez's name plus an order/batch code.
4. **Drawstrings on hoodies.** **Hood and neck drawstrings on children's upper outerwear in sizes 2T–12 are a "substantial product hazard."** Waist and bottom drawstrings in sizes 2T–16 must extend no more than 3 inches outside the channel, have no toggles or knots, and be bar-tacked if they're one continuous string ([CPSC drawstrings guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Business-Guidance/Drawstrings-in-Childrens-Upper-Outerwear); 16 CFR 1120). The **Gildan 18500B has "No drawcord at hood"** ([ShirtSpace G185B](https://www.shirtspace.com/gildan/sweatshirts/1222-gildan-g185b-youth-heavy-blend-hooded-sweatshirt)), so it's the right youth hoodie. Don't sell adult drawstring hoodies in kids sizes.
5. **POD providers' role.** Printful tells sellers to request a CPC from its Support team for kids' items ([Printful help](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/23375892288796-What-are-the-category-qualification-requirements-for-restricted-products-on-TikTok-Shop-US)). Collect CPCs for every kids SKU before launch.
6. **Accessories for younger kids.** Pin-backs and buttons have points. CPSC's sharp-point test applies to "toys and other articles intended for use by children under 8" ([16 CFR 1500.48](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-II/subchapter-C/part-1500/section-1500.48)). Market pins as **8+ or teen/adult**, or skip pins for the kids range.

*This isn't legal advice. For specifics, CPSC's Small Business Ombudsman is free: (888) 531-9070 ([CPSC lead page](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Lead/Total-Lead-Content)).*

## Recommendation

1. **Stay on POD.** Use **Printify's Gildan 5000B for kids tees** (cheapest, and it covers size 4). Use **Printful for hoodies (18500B), teen/adult tees and labels**. Sample both on black and white.
2. **Raise prices** to $22 kids / $26 teen / $30 adult, keep custom at +$10, and launch hoodies at $42 youth / $48 adult with the $6-off bundles.
3. **Reopen accessories in stages:**
   - Launch **stickers** (Sticker Mule, 50 minimum) and **totes and caps** (POD) first.
   - Sell **socks and button sets only as add-ons**, since they lose money when you pay the shipping.
   - Keep **enamel pins** for teen/adult (50 minimum).
4. **Get compliance in place before taking real orders:** CPCs from your providers, an inside tracking label, no-drawcord youth hoodies.
5. **Revisit bulk screen printing** only for a design that sells **100+ units** in kids sizes.
